CUSTOMER DATABASE - ENTITY RELATIONSHIP DIAGRAM (ERD)

TABLE: CUSTOMERS

Primary Key:
CustomerID

Columns:
1. CustomerID - Unique customer identifier
2. Name - Customer name
3. Age - Customer age
4. PurchaseAmount - Amount spent by customer
5. Country - Customer country
6. Date - Purchase date
7. PurchaseCategory - Low, Medium, or High

RELATIONSHIP:
The Customers table stores customer purchase information.
Each record represents one customer purchase.

DATABASE:
customer_database.db

TABLE NAME:
customers
